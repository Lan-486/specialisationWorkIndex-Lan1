Aquarium with Me — Parallel Prototypes

Home page Assignment 1

This page documents six parallel prototypes developed for Aquarium with Me, a browser-based audio instrument designed to create a calm and continuously changing underwater sound environment.

Design Area 1: Drag Interaction and Layout

This area explores the spatial relationship between the draggable sound elements and the aquarium drop zone. Each element represents a different sound layer, and placing it in the aquarium activates that sound. The interaction remains the same while the layout changes between the three prototypes.

Prototype A: Left-to-Right Layout

The draggable elements are placed on the left and the drop zone is placed on the right. This layout explores a clear left-to-right relationship between the controls and the aquarium.

Design Area 2: Random Bubble Generation

This area explores how bubbles are generated through the space bar. The prototypes use the extended technique Math.random() to vary bubble size, position, timing and movement.

Prototype 1: SingleRandom Bubble

Each press of the space bar creates a small random bubble. The bubbles appear with slight delays and use random values for their size, position and animation duration.
